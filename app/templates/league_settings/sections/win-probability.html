The leaderboard shows each fantasy player's estimated chance of winning the season, by simulating elimination orders for the remaining castaways. For each simulated outcome the full leaderboard is recalculated and the fantasy player who would win is tallied. The win percentage is how often each player comes out on top.

Prediction modes

Toggle these with the Projected switch in the leaderboard toolbar.

Frozen
DefaultUses each castaway's current stats as they stand. Only the elimination order is simulated.
Projected
Adds expected future statsBased on historical rates across all 50 US seasons. Castaways who survive longer in a simulation are projected to win more immunities and find more idols.

How projected rates work

Individual immunity wins are personalised: each castaway's rate comes from their career challenge history across every season they have played. Returning players use their full career stats; first-timers use the overall average of about 0.13 wins per post-merge tribal survived.

Idol and advantage rates use new-era seasons (41+) only, because the volume of both changed sharply in the modern game.

Idol protection: castaways holding an idol have a chance of playing it when they would be eliminated, surviving one more tribal, at the historical new-era rate.
