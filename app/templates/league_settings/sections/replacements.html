If one of your picks went out in or before the merge episode, you may select one Replacement from any castaway still in the game who is not already on your own team. Castaways on other players' teams are allowed, and two players can take the same one.

Eligibility

  • A drafted pick out in or before the merge episode earns you a full-strength replacement. That includes the castaway voted out at the first merged Tribal.
  • If only your Wildcard went out by then, you get a wildcard-strength replacement.
  • If none of your picks went out by the end of the merge episode, you are not eligible.
{% if replacement_self_service %}

Pick your Replacement yourself on My Team. You can change it as often as you like until picks lock 15 minutes before the first episode after the merge; after that only the league admin can enter one. Replacements stay hidden, and score no points, until every eligible player has picked.

{% endif %}

Scoring

{% if draft_repl_mult == 0 and wc_repl_mult == 0 %}

Replacement picks do not earn points this season. Our scoring analysis found that replacements rarely accumulate enough post-merge points to move the standings. They are a stay-in-the-game mechanic instead: you still get someone to root for after losing a pick early.

Replacements still appear on your roster and their stats are tracked; their point contribution is zero.

{% else %}

Replacements earn points only for what their castaway does from the moment you pick them. There are no retroactive points.

The merge is Episode 7 and your drafted castaway went out in Episode 5. Before Episode 8 you pick a replacement who has already survived 9 tribals and won an immunity. None of that counts. If they survive 4 more tribals and win, you score those 4 tribals plus any bonuses earned from Episode 8 on.

{% endif %}
