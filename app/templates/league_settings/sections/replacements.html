After the merge

If one of your picks was eliminated before the merge, you may select a Replacement from any remaining castaway not already on a roster.

Eligibility

  • A drafted pick eliminated pre-merge earns you a full-strength replacement.
  • If only your Wildcard went out pre-merge, you get a wildcard-strength replacement.
  • If none of your picks went out pre-merge, you are not eligible.

Scoring

{% if draft_repl_mult == 0 and wc_repl_mult == 0 %}

Replacement picks do not earn points this season. Our scoring analysis found that replacements rarely accumulate enough post-merge points to move the standings. They are a stay-in-the-game mechanic instead: you still get someone to root for after losing a pick early.

Replacements still appear on your roster and their stats are tracked; their point contribution is zero.

{% else %}

Replacements earn points only for what their castaway does from the moment you pick them. There are no retroactive points.

The merge is Episode 8 and your drafted castaway goes out in Episode 9. You pick a replacement who has already survived 9 tribals and won an immunity. None of that counts. If they survive 4 more tribals and win, you score those 4 tribals plus any bonuses earned after you selected them.
