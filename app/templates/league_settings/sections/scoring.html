Same-day tribals

Tribal survival counts per tribal council survived. When two or three castaways go home on the same night, they are all credited with the same number of tribals, so a double tribal does not punish whoever happened to be read out second.

Five tribals have happened and a double tribal sends two castaways home. Both are credited with 5 tribals. Everyone still in the game has now survived 6. On the leaderboard those two are labelled "Tied 5th voted out".
