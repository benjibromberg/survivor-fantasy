{# Why run 7 exists, shown in the Why fold of Scoring changes (changes.html). #}
  • Losing fire could beat making the final three. The castaway who loses fire finishes 4th and is a juror, worth 3. A finalist got 2 for Final Tribal plus half a point for one more tribal. Under that scoring, 4th place outscored at least one finalist in 5 of the 6 seasons from 45 to 50, and both finalists in Seasons 46 and 49.
  • Immunity wins scored nothing. Joe won four individual immunities in Season 48 and finished 3rd, and still scored less than the castaways who finished 4th and 5th.
  • Idols and advantages were out of order. Finding an advantage was worth half a point, finding an idol nothing, and playing an advantage nothing.
  • Sole Survivor picks are gone. The league doesn't use them, so they no longer score.

So the league set the rules below, and the scoring search only considered setups that keep them.
